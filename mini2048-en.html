🍬 How to Play

2048 AI Edition

🎯 Goal

Merge tiles all the way up to 2048! Keep going past 2048 — see how far you can push.

🕹 Controls

  • Mobile: swipe up / down / left / right on the board. Desktop: arrow keys / WASD to move, R to restart.
  • All tiles slide in the same direction. Tiles with the same number merge into a doubled tile.

  • After each valid move, a new tile (2 most of the time, occasionally 4) spawns randomly.
  • Each tile can only merge once per move — plan your route before sliding.

💡 Pro Tips

  • Pick a corner as your "home base" (e.g. bottom-left) and keep the largest tile there.
  • Keep rows/columns in descending order to leave room for movement.
  • Don't scatter large tiles — they'll trap you fast.

🏁 End & Scoring

  • Game over when the board is full and no moves are left in any direction.
  • Each merge adds the new tile's value to your score. Your best score is auto-saved.
